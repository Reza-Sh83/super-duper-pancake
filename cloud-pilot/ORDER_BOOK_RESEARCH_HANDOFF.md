### Frozen candidate and matched controls

- Use each CRC-valid top-of-book transition in one epoch. Let `b,a` be bid/ask prices, `B,A` their displayed quantities and subscript `prev` the previous accepted state. Level-one OFI is `1[b>=bprev] B - 1[b<=bprev] Bprev - 1[a<=aprev] A + 1[a>=aprev] Aprev`. Compute from displayed source depth before simulated consumption. The first snapshot seeds state. Rejected frames contribute nothing and reset the feature on invalidation. No cross-epoch change is scored.
- Sum OFI over the last five **complete** one-second receive-time buckets before a UTC five-second decision boundary. A bucket with verified continuous readiness and no updates contributes zero. Missing samples, invalid book, stale clock or metadata breaks readiness and requires fresh contiguous warm-up. Exclude arrivals at or after the decision boundary from the feature. On the first ready processing event at the boundary or within its following one second, emit at most one decision keyed by candidate version and boundary, otherwise record SKIP. Apply the same decisions/missing denominators across arms. Report actual receive-index cutoff and decision time. No retroactive decision or partial warm-up.
- Positive OFI targets 2% NAV, negative targets flat, and zero leaves the position unchanged. No short or passive orders. Matched price-only comparator uses the sign of the mid change across the same five-second interval, identical target policy and execution. Cash/no-order is the reference reconstruction and third arm. Freeze rules, horizon, source identity and all arms before viewing outcomes. Split any later research chronologically by independent windows. Purge intervals whose lookback, IOC, active exit or outcome horizon touch both partitions. Record an embargo that covers the remaining horizon, and fail closed if that horizon cannot be bounded. Keep this experiment out of confirmation.
- Independent ledger and consumed-depth state per arm, same immutable feed and instrument. Reuse the existing marketable limit, rounding, fee-inclusive sizing, IOC TTL, risk and persistent locks. Base 80 bp taker and 250 ms; stress 100 bp, 1,000 ms and adverse 10 bp with tick rounding. Match only on a strictly later eligible ready frame after latency, within the fixed limit and before five-second TTL (equality expires). Consume only available depth in price order and cancel the remainder. No depth replenishment from duplicate or unrelated updates. Existing caps and all invalid-source vetoes apply. Distinguish unavoidable spread, price improvement or adverse movement, fees and exit reserve in the report.
